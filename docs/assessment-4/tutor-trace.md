### The 10x Mindset: Documentation as Leverage

Source: lesson

The 10x Mindset: Documentation as Leverage
Learning objectives: Understand why top-tier developers treat documentation as a force multiplier rather than a chore. Define the psychological and practical barriers to documenting work.
Explanation: As a developer scaling toward senior and 10x impact, your bottleneck shifts from how fast you can type code to how effectively you can scale your knowledge. Documentation is asynchronous leverage. It prevents repetitive questions, captures context that code alone cannot express, and forms the bedrock of engineering autonomy.
Example: Instead of answering 'How do I run this service?' on Slack five times a week, a 10x developer writes a 5-minute setup guide once. When a new engineer onboards smoothly using that guide, the leverage of documentation is proven.
Practice: Audit your current workflow. Identify three tasks or explanations you repeated in the last two weeks that could have been documented.
Further reading: 'The Leprechaun Software Economics' by Rhett Morris.

Scaling Culture: Mentorship Through Documentation
Learning objectives: Use documentation as a scalable mentorship tool to level up your entire team and cement your 10x status.
Explanation: True 10x developers do not just multiply their own output; they multiply the output of everyone around them. By turning tacit knowledge into explicit documentation, you mentor engineers you have never met and scale your engineering culture across the organization.
